sum by (endpoint) (rate(capstone_requests_total[5m]))

## 2. Error rate percentage of all requests

100 * sum(rate(capstone_requests_total{status=~"4..|5.."}[5m])) / sum(rate(capstone_requests_total[5m]))

{"status":"success","data":{"resultType":"vector","result":[{"metric":{},"value":["1789495671.553","0"]}]}}

## 3. P95 latency

Query:

histogram_quantile(0.95, sum by (le) (rate(capstone_request_seconds_bucket[5m])))

Output:

{"status":"success","data":{"resultType":"vector","result":[{"metric":{},"value":["1789495674.035","0.06875"]}]}}

## Explanation

rate() calculates the per-second average increase of a Prometheus counter over the selected time range.

histogram_quantile() estimates a percentile from Prometheus histogram buckets. Here it is used to calculate the p95 request latency.
